Your project will be scored out of 10 in each of the following categories:

### Criteria 1: Innovation & Originality

How fresh, creative, and ambitious is your idea?
We want projects that feel thoughtful, bold, and non-obvious. This does not mean your idea has to be completely unheard of — but it should show originality in how you frame the problem, use AI, or build the solution.

### Criteria 2: Technical Complexity & Execution

How hard was the problem, and how well did you solve it?
This is a major part of judging. We care about both the ambition of what you attempted and the quality of what you actually built. A strong proof of concept can score very well if the technical core is real, convincing, and meaningfully executed.

### Criteria 3: Product Experience & Design

How clear, polished, and usable is your product?
A great project is not only powerful — it is also understandable. We care about how intuitive your product feels, how well the user flow works, and how clearly your demo communicates the value of what you built.

### Criteria 4: Impact & Practical Value

Could this matter in the real world?
We are looking for hacks that are blueprints for change — projects with the potential to
shape future generations and address humanity’s most pressing challenges in the years
ahead, whether through business value, societal benefit, or both.

### Track: Best Sustainability AI Hack

Awarded to the team that creates the most innovative AI solution to address environmental or sustainability challenges and drive meaningful real-world impact
